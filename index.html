Why wellness mattersKwa nini afya ni muhimu

Good health is rarely one big decision. It is many small choices repeated over weeks and months.

Afya njema mara chache hutokana na uamuzi mmoja mkubwa. Hutokana na maamuzi mengi madogo yanayorudiwa kwa wiki na miezi.

Six habits that matter mostMazoea sita muhimu zaidi

Eat wellKula vizuri

Choose whole foods, vegetables, fruit and balanced meals.

Chagua vyakula halisi, mboga, matunda na milo kamili.

Drink waterKunywa maji

Stay hydrated through the day.

Hakikisha unakunywa maji ya kutosha siku nzima.

MoveSonga

Walk, stretch or exercise a little every day.

Tembea, nyoosha mwili au fanya mazoezi kidogo kila siku.

RestPumzika

Aim for regular, good-quality sleep.

Lenga kupata usingizi wa kutosha na wa ubora kila usiku.

Stay consistentEndelea

Small habits repeated beat big plans that stop.

Mazoea madogo yanayorudiwa yanashinda mipango mikubwa inayokoma.

Ask a professionalMuulize mtaalamu

For any health condition or question, speak to your doctor or a qualified health professional.

Kwa tatizo au swali lolote la kiafya, zungumza na daktari au mtaalamu wa afya.

Nutritional supplements can be one part of a wider routine for some people. They do not replace a balanced diet, a healthy lifestyle or medical care.

Virutubisho vya lishe vinaweza kuwa sehemu ya ratiba pana kwa baadhi ya watu. Havichukui nafasi ya lishe kamili, mtindo bora wa maisha wala huduma ya kitabibu.
